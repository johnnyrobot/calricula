Research snapshot · September 2026 · California community colleges // applicationx × calricula — proposal

Put colleges in the placement business — with evidence, not guesswork.

ApplicationX is an AI-assisted companion to Calricula that finds the employers a program should be talking to, keeps those relationships alive, and feeds what employers actually need back into the Course Outline of Record. This deck pairs the owner's vision with a fact-checked look at the market, the data sources, and the compliance rules — and says plainly what is built, what is designed, and what is still a proposal.

01 / the problem — from the owner's source documents

Three audiences, one missing connection

Students and parents are losing faith in general education and want skills that lead to a job. Colleges that get good at placement will win — but they are not set up to do it.

The owner's Application Benefits deck and Benefit Summary frame the opportunity: colleges will need to get into the job-placement business, and that means knowing the employers in their region, engaging them, and matching students to them. The research below tests that framing against current external evidence and against what the two codebases already do.

Source of truth: Application Benefits.pptx (13 slides), ApplicationX Benefit Summary.docx, and Inputs and Outputs — Standard College Use Case.docx. Their text is not reproduced in the public repository.

for colleges

Deans and chairs are busy, understaffed, and blind to the region

  • Don't know all the employers that relate to their programs — some programs are fully online and statewide.
  • Don't know when employers open positions.
  • Struggle to engage employers in program improvement and hiring.
  • CTE deans have small clerical staff; chairs are often part-time from industry.
  • Carry heavy grant reporting (Strong Workforce, Perkins) on top of running programs.

for employers

They don't know which colleges produce qualified candidates — and don't fully trust the ones they know

  • Unaware of relevant programs, especially non-local ones.
  • Sifting through AI-written résumés to find interview-worthy candidates.
  • Don't see colleges as experts who can close their skills gaps.
  • No time to "donate" to a college unless it lowers the cost of hiring.

  • Trouble selecting a program of study.

  • Almost never have access to potential employers; online students are far from the region.

finding 1 · differentiation

The differentiator is real and uncontested

No product in the competitive set does employer-side discovery + CTE advisory-board workflow + curriculum feedback into a COR tool. Handshake, Symplicity, 12twenty and Lightcast Career Coach are student- and career-center-facing. Salesforce, Slate and Element451 are admissions CRMs. Steppingblocks is alumni analytics. Riipen supplies projects. Workforce Connect — the closest analogue — is a K-12 CTE partnership CRM with no AI discovery, LMI ranking, or COR loop.

verifiedsourced
§4 of the report
finding 2 · data sources

"Two years of postings per employer" has no cheap, legal path

Indeed retired its Publisher API in 2023. LinkedIn's Job Posting API is write-only and closed to new partners. Adzuna's free key is capped at 2,500 calls/month and its terms bar ongoing college or government use without written consent. CareerOneStop's Jobs API now sits behind the NASWA NLx Research Hub governance board. Scraping is barred by contract even where the CFAA does not apply (hiQ v. LinkedIn: $500K and a permanent injunction). Lightcast has the right facets — NAICS 2–6, SOC 2–5, O*NET, company, county/MSA — but no zip-code facet, and its pricing is unverified.

finding 3 · compliance

Grant reporting is the least-designed and most-specifiable benefit

CCCCO memo WED 25-80 defines exactly what a tool must capture for Perkins V: the LMI tests (regional median-of-median wage; 2024–2029 growth and openings ≥ regional median) and the stakeholder evidence (agendas, sign-in sheets, participant name/institution/title/phone/email — kept on file, never published). Appendix C explicitly accepts "real-time job postings data from online search engines" as evidence. Nothing in the PRD or spec mentions Perkins, Strong Workforce, CLNA, or advisory rosters.

finding 4 · privacy

Employer contacts are now a regulated asset in California

The Delete Act's DROP platform has been live since 1 Jan 2026; registered data brokers must poll it every 45 days from 1 Aug 2026 and propagate deletions to service providers. CalPrivacy has already fined B2B lead-generation vendors for non-registration. CPRA's B2B-contact exemption expired in 2023. A vendor-enriched "owner / HR director / supervisor" roster needs registry checks, deletion flow-down, and suppression — none of which the spec's Contact record carries today.

finding 5 · market

The value-of-college story is current and cites cleanly

Public confidence fell from 57% (2015) to 38% (2026); Americans trust community colleges more than four-year schools; only 56% of recent associate graduates say their degree was important to career goals vs. 80% of bachelor's graduates; ~70% of employers say new grads need moderate or substantial extra training. Only one in four employers say they communicate hiring needs transparently; 11% of educators report employers willing to set hiring targets.

sourced
Lumina-Gallup · HBS/AACC
finding 6 · the contrarian read

Discovery alone will not move placement

Strada's review of 109 college–employer partnership proposals found the bottleneck is employer capacity and college data infrastructure, not finding employers; fewer than half of learners get paid internships. Three-quarters of proposals included curriculum alignment and over 80% work-based learning — which is the loop ApplicationX instruments. The product has to carry the relationship, not just the list.

sourced
Strada
finding 7 · résumés

Generic AI résumé help adds to the flood

Applications per hire tripled 2021→2024 and stayed above 300; candidates are ~50% less likely to get an interview than five years ago. 80% of hiring managers say they can spot AI résumés and 62% say uncustomized ones are more likely rejected. Any résumé feature must produce personalised, employer-verifiable signals — not more boilerplate. (Note: the "AI apply tools" attribution in search snippets is not in Ashby's release.)

sourced
Ashby · Resume Now
finding 8 · system resources

California already has the LMI supplier — ingest it, don't rebuild it

The CCC Centers of Excellence are the system's LMI research arm: nine regional centers matching Strong Workforce consortia, customized occupation and sector data, employer surveys, and a system-level Lightcast licence. Strong Workforce outcomes on LaunchBoard come from EDD wage matching and the CTE Outcomes Survey — not from college tools. ApplicationX should consume these and supply the engagement inputs the state cannot see.

03 / the two platforms

Calricula owns the curriculum. ApplicationX owns the employer relationship.

calricula · curriculum management · public source

The catalog of record, with compliance built in

Faculty create, modify and route Course Outlines of Record and Programs through Faculty → Department → Committee → Articulation → Approved, with PCAH 8th ed. and Title 5 rules embedded in the UI.

  • COR authoring with AI suggestions (Gemini + File Search RAG).
  • 54-Hour Rule unit validation (Title 5 §55002.5), CB Code Wizard (27 Title 5 / PCAH codes), SLO editor with Bloom's distribution.
  • Labor-market data already wired: BLS OES wages, projections, QCEW county employment, California EDD.
  • Program builder (60-unit limit); roles for Faculty, Curriculum Chair, Articulation Officer, Admin.
  • Logto (OIDC) identity; light-only academic design; WCAG 2.2 AA required.
repo · builtNext.js · FastAPI · PostgreSQL
applicationx · companion service · private repo

Employer & Career Collaboration, inside Calricula and standalone

A separate FastAPI/PostgreSQL service with its own records and authorization. Staff open its workspace inside Calricula's layout; students and employers use a standalone entry. It exports evidence packages and proposed actions back to Calricula (curriculum) and Calipar (program review) — and can never approve a COR or decide a hire.

  • Built (P1): identity (Logto OIDC, dev tokens), organizations / workspaces / program mappings, the Calricula host-context handshake, row-level security, the connector contract with the Calricula labor-market connector, the workspace UI package and the standalone app.
  • Designed (P2): employer / location / contact / posting records, explainable relevance ranking, outreach drafts behind an approval bound to content and recipients, threads, meeting evidence, evidence graph, cross-app proposals.
  • Designed (P3): opt-in student ↔ employer contact, interview support, placement and alumni follow-up, private learning data.

What answers each finding today — and what we add

Find the employers a program should be talking to
"A continuously updated list of ALL employers in the region that might hire a graduate from any program." Inputs: known employers, NAICS, zip codes, job titles, SOC, O*NET Job Zones.
owner · slide 4 · benefit ¶1 · I/O Tier 1
designed · P2
what exists now

Calricula already joins programs to SOC codes and pulls BLS/EDD/QCEW wages, projections and county employment. ApplicationX J1 "Employer discovery and program evidence" specifies college + program revision + geography + occupations → cited brief → known employers + discovery from configured sources; every match shows location, roles, evidence, last observation, and why it appears. The spec keeps NAICS/SOC/CIP/TOP namespaces distinct and treats a zip code as a filter, not a Job Zone.

what the research says

The repo is right to refuse "ALL employers" (PRD §8). Named rosters need a licensed directory: Data Axle's government channel (~94M records with NAICS/SIC, size, executive titles; many CA college libraries already licence it) is the plausible source; Census County/ZIP Business Patterns gives free denominators ("how many employers of type X in region Y") but no names. Lightcast lacks a zip facet — plan county/MSA translation.

proposal

Pull P2-lite forward: the Employer / EmployerLocation / Contact / JobPosting migrations plus manual entry and the outreach-approval flow, all on $0 sources. Add Census ZBP as the coverage denominator and evaluate a Data Axle API licence for the named list. Say "employers in configured sources, with coverage shown" in stakeholder material.

Rank employers most likely to hire
"Prioritize employers in a ranking that identifies those most likely to hire the college's students and graduates."
owner · slide 4 · benefit ¶2
designed · P2
what exists now

AX-05 specifies an explainable relevance ranking that exposes its factors and is explicitly not a hiring-probability score. Acceptance tests cover duplicate branches, duplicate postings, required vs. desired skills and unknown history coverage.

what the research says

Consistent with the owner's intent once the deck stops saying "most likely to hire." The Perkins in-demand and high-wage tests give a defensible, state-recognised factor set (growth 2024–2029, openings ≥ regional median, wage ≥ regional median-of-median).

proposal

Make the Perkins tests first-class ranking factors and show them on each employer/occupation card with release date and region. Ingest the COE Occupation-in-Demand index rather than recomputing it.

Key contacts at each employer
"A list of key individuals at each employer location — VP of HR, line supervisors — with contact information."
owner · slide 4 · benefit ¶4 · I/O output 2
partial · P2
what exists now

The spec's Contact record carries "sourced professional contacts; consent/sharing state; no student data." Employer guests see only individually shared resources. No enrichment provider is selected.

what the research says

Delete Act registration ($6,000–6,600/yr; $200/day fines), DROP deletion polling every 45 days from 1 Aug 2026, deletion flow-down to service providers, and active CalPrivacy enforcement against B2B lead-gen vendors. Whether ApplicationX itself becomes a "data broker" if contacts are shared across colleges is unresolved.

proposal

Extend Contact with: source vendor + registry-check date, DROP suppression flag, email opt-out + timestamp, and an on-file-only vs. public-safe classification. Add a phase gate: no enrichment vendor without verified CalPrivacy registration and contractual deletion flow-down. Get a legal read on cross-college sharing before P2 ships.

Job postings, the moment they are published
"Notify dean, chairs, faculty and students of job postings from these employers the moment they are published"; "all job openings from each employer over the last (two?) years"; "minimum and desired qualifications extracted."
owner · slide 5 · benefit ¶11 · I/O outputs 3–4
scoped down · P2
what exists now

Spec §6 sets a daily configured monitor with "no promise of instant notifications or complete posting history"; closed postings get dated status observations rather than disappearing. JobPosting carries required/desired skill claims with extracted / inferred / confirmed status and a POSTING_REQUESTS_SKILL edge. J5 monitoring has an owner, last-run state and pause control.

what the research says

No free or self-serve source can supply the history (finding 2). NLx Research Hub (governed, daily/historical feeds) and Lightcast (2010→present, de-duplicated, skill-tagged, possibly reachable through the CCC system licence via COE) are the two viable paths. A daily digest still beats Lightcast Career Coach's monthly posting refresh.

proposal

File an NLx Research Hub request and ask COE whether the Lightcast system licence extends to ApplicationX — two quotes before P2. Until one is signed, change the deck line to "daily digest, with coverage and source shown." Keep the CLNA-ready posting summary (source, date range, count, limits) as a P2 output — it is explicitly accepted evidence.

Advisory boards, program review and accreditation
"Help department chairs recruit employers for annual program review meetings (required by accreditors)… allow asynchronous communication… help with the collection and presentation of data required for program review and accreditation."
owner · slide 4 · benefit ¶8
partial · P2
what exists now

ApplicationX J3 "Collaborative advisory review": the assistant compares scoped employer feedback and postings with the approved curriculum, drafts an agenda and gaps, links every claim to evidence; people confirm, assign owners and approve an action package that exports to Calricula (draft review item, never overwrites an approved COR) or Calipar (program review). This is the differentiator in finding 1.

what the research says

Title 5 §55601 prescribes committee composition (business, industry, EDD, students, faculty, administration, public). Ed Code §78016 requires a two-year CTE review against labor-market demand. The State Plan expects at least one advisory meeting per year. The CLNA wants agendas, sign-in sheets and participant lists on file. None of this roster/attendance structure is in the repo.

proposal

Add AX-14 "CTE compliance evidence" to P2: advisory roster with §55601 role categories and terms; structured meeting records with agenda artifact, minutes, sign-in capture and linked curriculum actions; a ≥1-per-year indicator; and a CLNA Element #6 export (narrative + evidence list, NOVA-ready PDF). This converts the owner's "recruit employers" benefit into the feature deans will ask about first.

Grant reporting: Perkins, Strong Workforce, Gainful Employment
"Possibly help colleges collect and report program data required by government regulators and accreditors… Perkins, Strong Workforce, Gainful Employment."
owner · slide 5 · benefit ¶16
absent
what exists now

Calricula already computes program-level LMI (wages, projections, county employment) and the ApplicationX lmi.lookup connector reads it by SOC. Nothing else: no Perkins, SWP, CLNA, LaunchBoard or NOVA reference anywhere in the PRD, spec or plans.

what the research says

Perkins V eligibility = two of three (high-skill / high-wage / in-demand) with exact thresholds published by CCCCO. SWP outcomes come from EDD wage match and CTEOS via LaunchBoard — a college tool cannot supply them, only the engagement inputs. Gainful Employment/FVT ends 30 Jun 2027 and is replaced by STATS and Earnings Accountability on 1 Jul 2027.

proposal

Encode the Perkins high-wage and in-demand booleans in lmi.lookup with region and release date; ingest COE / EDD regional projections as a source. Reframe the deck: Perkins CLNA evidence (yes), Strong Workforce engagement inputs (yes), employment/wage outcomes (link LaunchBoard, don't collect), "GE reporting" → STATS/earnings accountability.

Outreach from the college to employers
"A template letter, email or other communication that introduces the college's program and requests a phone or web conference call."
owner · I/O output 5
designed · P2
what exists now

AX-05 outreach drafts; external delivery requires an Approval bound to exact content hash, recipients, resource revision and expiry. Agents can draft but never send on their own. Imported content cannot grant agent permissions.

what the research says

If an email's primary purpose is commercial, CAN-SPAM requires accurate headers, a physical postal address, a conspicuous opt-out honoured within 10 business days, and ad identification; penalties run to ~$53K per email and sender liability cannot be contracted away. The blanket claim that college→employer partnership outreach is "commercial" was refuted 0–3 — so build the fields in by default, don't claim the law forces it.

proposal

Add postal address, opt-out link and a suppression list to the outreach template and the Contact record. Cheap, and it makes the college the good actor in every employer's inbox.

Students: résumés, interviews, follow-up, alumni
"Help students edit résumés, respond to applications and prepare for interviews… follow up after an interview… maintain relationships with graduates who land jobs… the foundation would also benefit."
owner · slide 5 · benefits ¶12–15
partial · P3
what exists now

AX-11 (P3): opt-in student/employer contact, applications, interview support, placement and alumni follow-up — private records excluded from the public graph, deletion within 24 hours, no employer access to raw grades. Public chat never queries student records.

what the research says

The résumé flood (finding 7) means generic AI résumé generation hurts students. FERPA's school-official exception governs any SIS import: direct control, purpose limitation, no redisclosure. Steppingblocks already sells alumni/advancement analytics to large universities; 12twenty's outcomes are survey-based and incomplete.

proposal

Reframe résumé help around employer-verified signals: competencies confirmed by the employer in the advisory loop (EMPLOYER_CONFIRMS_TASK) appear on the student's profile with the evidence trail. Keep interview-outcome capture as a structured P3 record. Park the foundation/advancement use until a pilot asks for it.

Employers: discover programs and shape curriculum without a campus visit
"Employers will discover what programs are offered… a more time-efficient way to communicate with colleges — to qualify candidates with faculty and to express their wishes for curriculum changes."
owner · slide 7
designed · P1/P2
what exists now

Employer feedback links to specific tasks, skills, objectives and assessments (J1 step 6) and flows into Calricula as a reviewable proposal (AX-09). This is the best-covered employer benefit. There is no employer-facing program discovery view yet; Calricula's approved catalog is the nearest existing surface.

what the research says

Over half of surveyed business leaders could not identify the skills they were hiring for; three-quarters of partnership proposals include curriculum alignment. Riipen (named by the owner) now embeds employer projects in coursework across 14–20 colleges in a Google.org/ACCT initiative launching fall 2026.

proposal

A P2 candidate: an employer-oriented occupation or skill → programs (statewide) view over Calricula's approved catalog. Treat Riipen as a P3 integration target timed to the fall-2026 cohort, with a WorkBasedLearning activity model (placement type, hours, supervisor, paid/unpaid).

Employer performance-gap consulting
"Help colleges identify gaps in each employer's performance metrics related to employee knowledge and training to potentially offer consulting services."
owner · slide 5 · benefit ¶7
absent
what exists now

Nothing in either repository.

what the research says

The owner's own slide 6 notes employers "do not see colleges as experts in this area." No external evidence supports demand for it, and no data source exposes employer performance metrics.

proposal

Park it. Revisit only when a pilot employer asks; the contract-education angle can ride on the advisory loop's skills-gap output if it does.

// how the pieces fit

Calricula stays the record. ApplicationX becomes the reason employers show up.

Calricula already validates units, codes and outcomes and pulls the labor-market numbers. ApplicationX adds the employers, the conversations, and the evidence trail — then hands a reviewable proposal back to the curriculum workflow. AX-14 closes the loop by making that same evidence the college's Perkins and program-review record, so the compliance work deans dread becomes a by-product of the engagement work they want.
