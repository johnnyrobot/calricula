--- ppt/slides/slide2.xml ---
A New Value Proposition for Higher Ed
More and more students (and parents) are losing faith in the value of traditional higher education.
They are becoming less interested in general liberal arts education. Instead, the goal seems to be building skills for a good career and landing a great job.
In the future, colleges will be more successful if they can get better at delivering this value to students.
To do so, colleges will need to get more involved in the job placement business.  It will no longer be good enough to prepare students with knowledge and skills; colleges will need to work harder to get students into the careers/jobs they are seeking and making a good match between student and employer.
--- ppt/slides/slide3.xml ---
What problems are we trying to solve for colleges?
Colleges don’t know all the employers in their region that relate to the programs they offer. And some programs are fully online and statewide.
Colleges don’t always know when employers open positions
Colleges have trouble engaging employers in efforts to improve their programs and employ their graduates
Career and technical education (CTE) deans are notoriously busy with small clerical staff
Faculty department chairs are often part-time (from industry) and are also quite busy
Adding to the usual workload of running academic programs, CTE deans and chairs have considerable work in getting and reporting on the state and federal grants available to them (e.g., Strong Workforce, Perkins)
--- ppt/slides/slide4.xml ---
ApplicationX: Benefits for Colleges
Provide a continuously updated list of ALL employers in the college’s region that might wish to hire a student or graduate from any of the college programs (AI can do this)
Based on criteria provided by AI and/or dean/chairs, prioritize employers in a ranking that identifies those most likely to hire the college’s students and graduates. 
Help automate or assist college staff in creating and maintaining relationships with employers
Provide a list of key individuals at each employer location
Continuously comb the Internet for articles, news releases and other info about each employer
Provide means of asynchronous communication between college, employers and (perhaps) students
Help department chairs recruit employers for annual program review meetings (required by accreditors) to help keep program up-to-date with industry changes
--- ppt/slides/slide5.xml ---
ApplicationX: Benefits for Colleges (cont.)
Possibly assist deans and program chairs in collecting and analyzing available labor market data to create and improve programs 
Notify dean, chairs, faculty and students of job postings from the employers the moment they are published
Allow college staff to better help students edit resumes, respond to applications, and prepare for interviews (leverage/amplify the work of college’s career services unit)
Allow college staff to easily follow up after a student or graduate completes an interview
Help colleges identify gaps in each employer’s performance metrics related to employee knowledge and training to potentially offer consulting services
Help colleges maintain relationships with graduates who land jobs in chosen field
Possibly help colleges collect and report program data required by gov’t regulators and accreditors (e.g., post-program employment/wage info)
--- ppt/slides/slide6.xml ---
What problems are we trying to solve for employers?
Employers don’t always know which colleges are offering programs that might produce job candidates qualified to fill positions (some not local)
They don’t always trust those colleges they know to deliver qualified candidates with sufficient skills
They face the challenge of sifting through resumes (often written by AI) trying to find candidates qualified for interview
They don’t always know where their performance gaps are (those caused by lack of knowledge/skills) and they do not see colleges as experts in this area, able to help improve their performance
They are very busy and focused on business outcomes – not much time to “donate” to a college unless it provides an ROI (lower the cost of hiring)
--- ppt/slides/slide7.xml ---
ApplicationX: Benefits for Employers
Employers will discover what programs are offered by colleges using our app (even those outside their local region)
They can build trust with a source for qualified candidates
The app can help them find and hire “good match” qualified candidates more quickly and efficiently, reducing the cost of hiring
They will have a more effective and time-efficient way to communicate with colleges--to qualify candidates with faculty and to express their wishes for curriculum changes (without having to travel to a campus)
They will discover that college faculty and instructional designers can help with their manpower and training needs
--- ppt/slides/slide8.xml ---
What problems are we trying to solve for students?
Students don’t always know what job/career they want
Even if they do, they sometimes  don’t have access to a college career center’s services until after they enroll
They have trouble selecting any particular program of study
Some college career development services are not available fully online
Some students may wish to explore careers/jobs outside their current college’s geographic district
Students often do not have easy access to any college faculty (other than current teachers) and almost never have access to potential employers
Students in fully online programs have an added challenge finding employment in their field of study because they may not live in region
--- ppt/slides/slide9.xml ---
ApplicationX: Benefits for Students
Colleges can encourage students to engage earlier with the career center to select a career path and related educational program
Students can access career development services online (if that is not already offered by their college)
Students can potentially have access to job boards and labor market data to help identify career opportunities in ANY area of the U.S.
Students will have access to communication tools to help them engage with college program faculty and (potentially) with employers
The application can help students find apprenticeships, internships, and other forms of work-based learning
